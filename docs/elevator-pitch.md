# Vachan — 60-second elevator pitch

Every day, freelancers and small businesses in India make important promises in chat: “I’ll send the payment when it’s done.” The problem is not a lack of payment rails. The problem is that payment rarely explains what the money was for, what “done” means, or what evidence should resolve a disagreement.

Vachan is the trust layer between a work promise and a payment. It turns informal agreements into verifiable milestones with clear scope, payment status, proof of work, acceptance, disputes, and tamper-evident receipts.

Vachan works around existing rails like UPI and Stripe Checkout. It never holds customer funds or card details. Stripe collects the payment, and Vachan only records verified, signed webhook events in the timeline.

We are not replacing India’s payment infrastructure. We are making the work around it trustworthy.
