PAYMENT BOUNDARY / 03

Best-in-class rails.
Clear boundaries.

Vachan never becomes a wallet or an escrow account. The server creates a provider-hosted Checkout Session from the stored milestone amount, then listens for signed events.

Read the principles →
01Provider-hosted

Stripe collects card details. Vachan receives only the signed result.

02Idempotent

Duplicate webhooks are recorded once by Stripe event ID.

03Test-first

Local environments stay disabled until explicit test keys are configured.

DESIGNED FOR TRUST / 04

Not a bank.
Not a black box.

Vachan coordinates work around the payment rails India already trusts—not replace them.

01

No custody

Your money stays with your payment provider. Vachan records intent, status, and evidence.

02

Receipts with context

A receipt tells the story of the deal, not only the amount and transaction ID.

03

Human first

Automation accelerates review, but a person remains in control of acceptance and disputes.
